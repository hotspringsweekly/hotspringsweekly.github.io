Local news

Heads up: Hot Springs Weekly's publisher, Brenden Anglin, works for the City of T or C. Our city coverage sticks to public records, with no opinions.

  • T or C City Commission (Oct 28): on the agenda were combining the lots at 401 and 403 Silver St. into one (the owner plans to come back later for a building), $65,250 from the fire fund for new breathing gear (SCBA) for the fire department, and change orders on the city's water system work. The city's new refuse truck order also went up $5,000 because of a delay, from $390,000 to $395,000. The minutes aren't posted yet, so check them for the final votes. City agenda packet, Oct 28
  • A film shoot downtown Nov 10 and 11: a cybersecurity company called Axonius plans to film its online event, Adapt Virtual 26, in downtown T or C on Tuesday and Wednesday, according to the Oct 28 commission packet. It also asked to ceremonially "rename" the town Axonius for one day, Dec 9, when the recording airs. City agenda packet, Oct 28
  • Cantrell Dam is a city project. The City of T or C maintains the dam and is leading the work to fix it, with help from the Sierra County Flood Commissioner. On Oct 20 the Sierra County Commission voted to chip in $200,000 toward construction, since flooding there affects the fairgrounds, Williamsburg and nearby neighborhoods too. County Resolution 2026-67
  • Sierra County Commission (Oct 20): a resolution on the agenda names the Sugar Sand Bridge the county's No. 1 infrastructure priority. The county has about $6.84 million lined up for the $8.68 million bridge and is short about $1.84 million. Commissioners also had a suggested data center study and an update on the September flooding on the agenda. County meeting packet, Oct 20
  • Tigers football: they finished the regular season 5-5 after a 34-20 loss to Ruidoso and earned the No. 10 seed in the 3A state playoffs. First round is Saturday, Nov 7 at noon at No. 7 West Las Vegas (7-3). The winner faces No. 2 Hope Christian in the quarterfinals Nov 13-14. Midnight Horizons Sports, Nov 1
  • Tiger volleyball is 19-3 and 5-0 in district after sweeping Socorro 3-0 on Tuesday. They play at Cobre tonight (Thursday) at 7. MaxPreps schedule
  • Mosquito spraying: the city has extended its mosquito spraying through Nov 14. City of T or C
  • Free Dump Day, Fri and Sat, Nov 13 and 14, 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. Sierra County's transfer stations in Winston, Hillsboro and Arrey take household trash, construction debris and recyclables for free both days. No tires. The county also reminds folks that illegal dumping on public lands can bring a fine of up to $5,000. (Sierra County flyer)
  • County offices closed Wed, Nov 11 for Veterans Day. Sierra County 2026 holiday calendar
  • Shooting range closed Fri, Nov 21, 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. The city says Rio Vaqueros has a maintenance workday out there, so the range is closed to everyone that day. City alerts
  • Next meetings: T or C City Commission on Wed, Nov 18 at 9 a.m., 405 W. Third St. (Oct 28 agenda). The County Commission hasn't posted its November agenda yet. Watch for it here.

Out at the Spaceport

Virgin Galactic's new spaceship finally came home. On Wednesday, Oct. 28, the ship they're calling Apeiron flew in from Arizona, hooked under the mothership Eve, and landed at Spaceport America at 12:59 p.m. Now comes testing. Virgin says paying customers should start flying in early spring 2027 (its "early Q2"), with a second ship following in late spring or early summer. That date has slipped before. It used to be the end of this year. Virgin Galactic, Oct 28 Virgin reports its third-quarter numbers after the markets close Thursday, Nov 12 (Virgin Galactic), and the Albuquerque Journal reports the company plans to share more about testing then. It's been about two years since Virgin last flew from here (Albuquerque Journal).

  • Tours: Saturdays at 9 a.m. from the Geronimo Trail Visitor Center, 523 N. Broadway (be there by 8:40). You're back around 1. It's $49.99 for adults, and they need at least 7 people for the tour to run. Book here
  • Watching a launch: launches aren't open to the public. We'll pass along dates when they're announced.
  • Hiring: the Spaceport has an engineer opening. See 5 jobs hiring this week below.
  • Road heads-up: the Spaceport Authority asked the county to close county road A039 (about half a mile from A013) and Lewis Road for unmanned aircraft testing on Nov 4, 7 and 10. The remaining windows are Saturday the 7th, 9 to 11:30 a.m., and Tuesday the 10th, 11:30 a.m. to 2 p.m. County meeting packet, Oct 20

Lake levels and fishing

  • Elephant Butte: 4,306.6 feet on Tuesday, Nov 3, holding about 154,800 acre-feet. That's roughly 8 percent full, and the lake has come up about 9 feet since Oct 26. (Bureau of Reclamation) Catfish were slow to fair on cut shad in the last Game & Fish report. The main ramp at Marina del Sur is open. State Parks' ramp list (last updated Oct 26) shows Rock Canyon and South Monticello closed, so call before you head to either one. The park says you need four-wheel drive to launch or pull out, and to watch for debris and shallow spots. Call ahead: 575-744-5923. (State Parks alerts)
  • Caballo: 4,139.0 feet, about 25,850 acre-feet, on State Parks' Oct 26 update. The west ramp is open and the east ramp is closed. Water levels have been changing since the flooding, so go easy in low spots. Park: 575-743-3942. (State Parks alerts)
  • Fishing notes are from the Game & Fish Oct 29 report, the latest one out as of Wednesday night.

Free help for local families

We give these spots away free to local folks who help kids and families. Nobody paid to be here.

Healthy Families Sierra County. Expecting, or have a little one at home? They'll come to you. They do free home visits from pregnancy through the early years and can point you toward food, housing or health care help. It's voluntary and confidential. 400 N. Broadway, T or C · (575) 223-2585 · info@hfsierracounty.com

Little Things Matter (AppleTree Educational Center). Free home visits for families from pregnancy up to age 3. They check how your baby is developing, help with car seats and breastfeeding, and have a dads' group too. English and Spanish. (575) 894-5646

Aprendamos Early Intervention. Worried your baby or toddler is behind on talking, walking or other milestones? Aprendamos works with kids from birth to 3, mostly right in your home, and it's free for families. Call their main line at (575) 526-6682 · info@aitkids.com

T or C Schools Family Resource Center. If your kids go to T or C schools and you're short on supplies, clothes or other basics, start here. (575) 894-8166

Matthew 25 Food Pantry. Free groceries every Thursday from 9 to 11 a.m. at 405 Austin St. Signing up is quick and you don't need ID. They can always use volunteers and food donations. torcmatthew25foodpantry@gmail.com

Above the Hot Springs

What's up in our sky, Nov 5 to 12.

  • Moon: a waning crescent to start the week, then the new moon is Monday, Nov 9 at 12:02 a.m. our time. After that it's a growing crescent in the evening, and first quarter is Tuesday, Nov 17. (U.S. Naval Observatory)
  • Sunset in T or C: 5:14 p.m. Thursday, Nov 5, and 5:09 p.m. by Thursday, Nov 12. (U.S. Naval Observatory)
  • Evening planet: Saturn is the one bright planet up after sunset, in the east-southeast. (When the Curves Line Up)
  • Morning planets: step out an hour before sunrise for bright Venus low in the east-southeast, with Jupiter and reddish Mars higher in the south-southeast. Mars and Jupiter get about 1.2 degrees apart on the mornings of Nov 15 and 16. (When the Curves Line Up, NASA)
  • Meteors: the Southern Taurids peaked the night of Nov 4 to 5, and the Northern Taurids peak the night of Nov 11 to 12. They only give you about five an hour at best, but they're known for bright fireballs. The Leonids start up Nov 6 and peak the night of Nov 16 to 17. (American Meteor Society, NASA)
  • Best night to look up: Wednesday, Nov 11, into Thursday morning. It's the Northern Taurid peak, and the moon is only about 4 percent lit, so the sky stays dark. Get away from porch lights and give your eyes half an hour to adjust. (American Meteor Society)
  • Rockets: for Spaceport news, see Out at the Spaceport above.

Did you know?

Back in June 2014, a stegomastodon fossil turned up at Elephant Butte Lake State Park. That's an ancient relative of today's elephants, found right at a lake named for a butte that looks like one. (Wikipedia)
